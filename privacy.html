iFruit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 September 2026

iFruit is an offline fruit-plant library published by We The Fruit. This policy explains how the Android edition handles information.

What the app stores

The plant library is bundled in the app. It is not fetched from our servers at runtime.

If you create a local profile, the email you enter, a salted password hash, and saved taxa are stored on this device. We do not receive this information. Android may include local app data in the operating system backup associated with your Google account, subject to your device and Google backup settings.

What we do not collect

This edition does not include analytics, advertising, crash telemetry, a public export function, or an app network permission. We do not operate a cloud account server in this edition.

Your choices and deletion

You can use the library without creating a local profile. To remove local profiles, saved taxa, and other app state, clear iFruit storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. You can also control Android backup in your device or Google account settings.

Children

iFruit is a general reference library and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Local profile information is not sent to us.

Contact

Corrections and support: ppzbbb@163.com

iFruit is not medical or dietary advice. Scientific names, distributions, and citations remain original source records. The map colours countries; it is not a precise locality or range boundary.
